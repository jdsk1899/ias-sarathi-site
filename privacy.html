IAS Sarathi

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

IAS Sarathi (“the app”, “we”, “us”) helps people prepare for the UPSC Civil Services Preliminary Examination. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have. It applies to the app on iPhone and Android.

We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your data.

1. Using the app as a guest

You can use IAS Sarathi without an account. As a guest, the app creates an anonymous account for you. It has no name, email or phone number. Your progress is saved under it, on your phone and on our server, as listed in section 2.

A guest account can only be reached from the phone it was made on. If you delete the app, you lose access to it. Create an account if you want to keep your progress. You can delete a guest account in the same way as any other account (section 10).

2. What we collect

Sign-in details If you create an account: your email address and the way you sign in (email and password, Google or Apple). Google and Apple may also share your name. With Apple you can choose to hide your email, and we then get a private relay address. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see them.
Your profile in the app The name and picture style you choose. If you add them, your date of birth, which attempt this is, and your optional subject. These are private to your account.
Your study progress The questions you answer and whether you got them right, your study timer sessions, mock test results and answer sheets, mistakes kept for review, your streak and shields, goals, reminder times and settings. We back these up so you can sign in on another phone and keep everything.
Your notes The notes and notebooks you write, and any photos you add to a note.

3. Discuss and study circles

Discuss lets you ask and answer questions inside study circles. If you use it, we store:

  • Your public profile: your handle, display name, short bio and picture style. Other signed-in users can see it.
  • The circles you create or join, and invitations and requests to join.
  • The questions and replies you post, images you attach, and your votes. Members of the circle can see them.
  • Posts you save, people you block, and your in-app notifications. These are private to you.

Please don’t post personal details, such as a phone number or address, in Discuss.

4. Reports

You can report an error in a question, or report a post or reply. A report records your account ID, what you reported, the reason you chose and when. Moderators use it to fix questions and keep circles safe.

5. Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled on your phone. They do not send anything to us.

If you allow notifications, the app also stores a push token for your phone. We use it to tell you about replies, invitations and other Discuss activity. The token is sent through Expo’s push service to Apple or Google, who deliver the notification. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone’s settings.

6. Purchases

IAS Sarathi Premium is a subscription sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google take the payment. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.

We use RevenueCat to check and restore subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your purchase records from the store, linked to your app account ID, and tells our server which plan you have and when it ends.

7. Who handles your data

We use these service providers to run the app. Each one handles data only to provide its service to us.

Google Firebase Sign-in, our database, file storage for photos and images, and the server code that runs the app’s features.
RevenueCat Subscriptions and purchase records.
Expo Delivering push notifications.
Apple and Google Sign in with Apple, Google sign-in, payments, and push notification delivery.

These providers may store and process data on servers in India and in other countries. We do not share your personal data with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

8. What stays on your phone

The question bank, current affairs, your settings and a copy of your progress are kept on your phone, so the app works offline. Downloading questions and current affairs is an ordinary web request, so the server sees your phone’s IP address, as any website would.

9. How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile, handle, study progress, notes, photos, uploaded images, circle memberships, invitations, requests and push tokens are deleted.
  • Questions and replies you posted in Discuss stay in their threads so other members’ answers still make sense, but your name and handle are removed and they show as “Deleted account”. Images you attached are deleted. You can delete any post yourself before deleting your account.
  • Reports you made may be kept, so that moderators can finish acting on them.

If you delete the app while using a guest account, that account can no longer be reached from any phone. Its progress stays on our server, linked to no name or email.

Some copies may remain in our service providers’ systems for a short time before they are removed.

10. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data. Your progress, notes and profile are all shown in the app, and you can edit or delete them there.
  • Delete your account. In the app, go to Profile → Backup & sync → Delete my account and all data. You can also follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Withdraw consent. You can turn off notifications, stop using Discuss, or delete your account at any time.
  • Ask us. Email us to ask what data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it erased, or to name someone to act for you. We will reply within 15 days.
  • Complain. If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact our Grievance Officer (below). You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Security

Data travels to and from our servers over encrypted connections. Our database rules allow each person to read and change only their own private data, and circle content only for the circle’s members. No system is perfectly secure, but we will tell you and the authorities if a breach affects your data, as the law requires.

12. Children

IAS Sarathi is meant for adults preparing for the Civil Services Examination. You must be 18 or older to create an account. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data in an important way, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
