Delete your IAS Sarathi account

You can delete your account and its data at any time. It takes a minute in the app.

In the app

  1. Open IAS Sarathi and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Backup & sync.
  3. Tap Delete my account and all data at the bottom.
  4. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in again, or enter your password, first.

Your account is deleted at once.

Without the app

If you can’t open the app, email jaydeepsinghkaushik@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with. Use the subject “Delete my IAS Sarathi account”. If you signed in with Apple and hid your email, tell us the address the app showed you. We will confirm the request and delete your account within 15 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in details, profile, handle and picture.
  • All your study progress: answers, streak, mock results, mistakes, timer sessions, goals and settings.
  • Your notes and notebooks, and photos in them.
  • Images you attached in Discuss, your circle memberships, invitations and requests, saved posts, blocks and notifications.
  • Your phone’s push token.

What is kept

  • Questions and replies you posted in Discuss stay in their threads so other members’ answers still make sense. Your name and handle are removed, and they show as “Deleted account”. You can delete your posts yourself before deleting your account.
  • Reports you made about questions or posts may be kept, so moderators can finish acting on them.
  • A circle you own passes to the member who has been in it longest. If nobody else is in it, it is deleted.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel IAS Sarathi Premium. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, or you will keep being charged.

Guest accounts

If you use the app as a guest, the same steps in the app delete the guest account and its progress. A guest account has no email, so it can only be deleted from the phone it was made on.
